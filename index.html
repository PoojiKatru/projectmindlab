Sessions

Mindlab sessions are designed to transform complex academic concepts into engaging, interactive learning experiences for students in grades 6–9. Using the Mindlab book as a foundation and guide, we introduce each topic in a way that is accessible, relatable, and enjoyable for students.

Rather than relying on traditional lectures, our sessions will prioritize active participation. Concepts from the book will be reinforced through interactive discussions, hands-on activities, games, experiments, challenges, and real-world scenarios. Students will have opportunities to ask questions, work collaboratively, test ideas, and apply what they learn beyond the page.

Our goal is to make each session both educational and memorable by presenting challenging concepts in a way that feels approachable and fun. By combining the structured content of the book with creative and engaging activities, Mindlab aims to create an environment where students are encouraged to think critically, explore new ideas, and actively participate in their own learning.

Beneath The Choice

In progress.

Mindlab is designed to provide students in grades 6–9 with opportunities to strengthen their critical thinking, financial literacy, economic knowledge, and statistical skills.

The program's book serves as a foundational resource for both volunteers and students. Using clear, easy-to-understand explanations, practical examples, and practice questions, it introduces students to essential concepts in an engaging and accessible way.

The book is organized into seven sections covering a total of 30 topics. Students explore topics such as evidence, statistics, probability, decision-making, and cognitive biases while developing the skills needed to evaluate information and make informed decisions. Activities and experiments throughout the book encourage students to apply what they learn in real-world situations.

Mindlab's curriculum is designed not only to introduce important concepts but also to help students understand how these ideas influence everyday decisions. By using the book as a guide, volunteers can provide students with a structured and interactive learning experience that builds practical knowledge and critical thinking skills.

About Us

Shreya L. Co-Founder Junior at Kennedy High School

Hey! I’m Shreya, a junior at Kennedy High School and a co-founder of MindLab. I’m interested in the space where math, economics, and human behavior meet: why people make the decisions they do, how we judge risk, and how easily numbers can influence what we believe. I started thinking more about these topics after realizing that statistics and probability are often taught as formulas, even though they are factored in constantly when making choices in real life. That very idea became the foundation for MindLab and the book I co-wrote, Beneath the Choice. We built it around questions that go beyond simply getting the right answer, like how economic incentives shape the choices people make. My hope is that middle and early high-school students come away more willing to question information, trust their own reasoning, and see math as a tool for understanding the world rather than just a class to get through. In my free time, I’m usually reading, looking for a new coffee shop, or going on a hike.
